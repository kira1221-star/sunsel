About Sunsel Pharma

Focused on pharmaceutical and medical-equipment supply

Sunsel Pharma PLC is an Ethiopia-based business in Addis Ababa. Its publicly listed activities include importing and wholesaling medicines and medical equipment.

This website provides a point of contact for pharmacies, healthcare organizations, and other businesses seeking product information or a quotation. Product availability and details should be confirmed directly with the company.
